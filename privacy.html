Privacy Policy

Bills doesn't collect, store or share any personal data.

  • Your expenses, currency, language and settings are saved only on your device.
  • The app has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking, and it doesn't send your data to any server.
  • Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device.
  • Export creates a file that you choose where to save or share. Import reads only the file you pick.

Deleting the app deletes all of its data from your device.
